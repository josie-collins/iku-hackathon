# Budget Group Trip Planner

**Tagline:** Plan together. Spend privately. Settle simply.

**Track:** Wise – Max Ease

## Inspiration

We've all seen group trips get complicated before they've even started. One person wants a weekend away, another is trying to save money, and someone else has to coordinate everyone's schedules. Eventually the conversation turns to budgets, and not everyone feels comfortable saying what they can afford.

We wanted to make these conversations easier. Travelling with friends should be about spending time together, not about worrying whether you can afford the same activities or feeling pressured to explain your finances.

## What it does

Budget Group Trip Planner helps groups organise trips around everyone's needs without asking anyone to reveal their personal budget.

Each person privately enters their availability, departure location, travel preferences and three budgets: one for their own flights, a group budget for shared costs like accommodation, and a personal budget for food and extras. The app uses this to find destinations and trip options that work for the whole group, while keeping individual spending limits private. Nobody ever sees anyone else's numbers.

Once the group has voted on a destination and a stay, the app turns everyone's choices into a clear budget plan. The group sees the shared cost per person: their share of the accommodation and of the activities everyone voted for. Each person also gets a private breakdown of their own budget: what's already committed to shared costs, an estimate for food based on the destination and their dietary preferences, and how much is left for anything else they want to do. Activities that only some people want become optional, so everyone can join what fits their budget without explaining why they're skipping something. Whenever the group's choices change, the plan updates.

The app also helps during and after the trip. Users log expenses with receipt photos or voice input, marking each as shared, an optional extra, or personal. Every evening the app nudges everyone to log what they paid for that day, and shows each person a private update on their own budget, such as "You're €18 under your daily pace", with a warning if they're overspending. The group sees the shared spending, while personal budgets stay private. At the end, the app works out who owes whom in each person's own currency. Settling up across currencies becomes much simpler, with Wise supporting the currency conversion and payment handoff where the integration allows.

We use AI where it is genuinely helpful: reading receipts, understanding spoken expenses, suggesting itineraries and adapting plans when circumstances change. Budget matching, privacy rules and expense calculations are handled by application logic, not left to an AI model.

What makes the idea different is that privacy is part of planning from the very beginning. Instead of only splitting costs after a trip, we help groups find a trip they can all feel comfortable joining in the first place.

## What's next for Budget Group Trip Planner

Our first priority is a reliable end-to-end experience, from private budgets and trip matching through to a working expense settlement flow.

From there, we want the planner to be more adaptable. If a flight becomes too expensive, accommodation is no longer available or the weather changes, the app should suggest alternatives and explain what has changed.

We also want to make the experience more accessible through voice-based expense logging and multilingual interactions, and to use spending history to give users a clearer picture of their expenses over time.

In the longer term, we'd like to explore more direct travel integrations and a smoother Wise payment experience, alongside better ways to support groups whose members have very different budgets.

Our goal is simple: make it easier for people to travel together without money becoming an uncomfortable part of the friendship.
